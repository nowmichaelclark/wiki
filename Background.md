# Background

Background articles explain the general subjects behind the projects: real-time audio, synthesis, music theory, physics, game design and so on. They are written like good Wikipedia articles, neutral, sourced and readable by a newcomer, and they say nothing specific about our own code. How Driftloom or Broken Friends uses an idea belongs on the project's own pages, which link here.

Most background articles are written by DeepSeek with the method in [[DeepSeek article guide|Wiki/DeepSeek article guide]], then checked in a second chat. Claude may also write them, following the same guide. Every background article must stay Discord-safe: the Download for Discord button on each page turns it into a file that Discord's Markdown viewer shows correctly.
