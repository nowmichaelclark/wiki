# Workshop Wiki

This wiki covers the sound, game and toy software Mikey makes with Claude: what each project is, how its parts work, why things were decided the way they were, and the background knowledge behind them. Everything here is meant to run fast on cheap, old hardware, and so is the wiki.

## Projects

- [[Driftloom]]: a procedural loop machine that generates and synthesizes relaxing loops in the browser.
- [[Broken Friends]]: a 2D, mobile-first creature-care game made of many small physics mini-games.

## Background

[[Background]] holds encyclopedia-style articles on the subjects the projects stand on, such as real-time audio, synthesis, physics and game design. They are sourced, written in the style of Wikipedia, and can be exported for Discord. [[Wanted articles|Background/Wanted articles]] lists the ones still to write.
